## Key Economic Figures
- **Training Costs:** $10M – $100M+ for frontier models.
- **Energy Arbitrage:** 50–80% reduction via geographic optimization.
- **Software/Algorithmic Efficiency:** 2–10x improvement.
- **Data Efficiency:** 2–5x reduction in required training tokens.
- **Inference Bottleneck:** Memory bandwidth is the primary constraint, not raw compute.
- **Ecosystem Effects:** 30–60% reduction in effective costs.
- **Platform Revenue:** 20–40% expansion revenue.
- **Network Effects:** 15–25% retention increase; 30–50% CAC reduction.
- **Compliance Cost:** 5–15% of project budget.
- **Accessibility Gain:** +15–20% addressable market expansion.
- **Model Sizing:** 7B–34B models often suffice for educational tasks vs. 100B+ frontier models.

## The Educational AI Value Equation
`Educational Value per Investment = [Learning Outcomes × Reach × Retention × Expansion Potential] / [Direct Costs + Systemic Costs - Systemic Benefits + Regulatory Costs - Regulatory Benefits]`

## Summary Thesis
The viability of AI in education is measured not in tokens consumed, but in minds enlightened. Success requires optimizing across the training-inference nexus, leveraging ecosystem effects, and turning regulatory compliance into a strategic differentiator.
