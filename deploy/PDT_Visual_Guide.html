Step 2: The Visual Grammar

Once the scene is loaded, use this key to interpret the Logic Layer conduits.

Economic Indicators

Gold/Amber: Private Investment Flow (FDI, Commercial Capital)
Cyan/Blue: Policy Funding (Government Grants, Zoning)
Violet/Magenta: Knowledge Capital (University Spillover)
Flow Dynamics:
• Thickness: Volume of GDP contribution.
• Velocity: Speed of capital turnover.
• Pulse: Smooth = Sustainable | Jagged = Speculative.

Inspection Checklist

  • The Knowledge Loop: Trace Violet flows from the University of Pretoria.
  • Policy Priming: Find Cyan flows triggering Gold investment clusters.
  • Economic Deserts: Find high-density roads with no neon flows.
